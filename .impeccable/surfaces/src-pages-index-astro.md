# DEWY CO. storefront

Mode: Persuade. Scope: /beauty-brand, complete premium beauty discovery demo. User selected The Beauty Atelier and interactive-demo scope. Code-led: no image-generation tool available. Local Pexels photography only; stock packaging is illustrative. No medical claims, fake endorsements or checkout.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Personal beauty as a contemporary fitting salon. Campaign photography and a tactile ritual finder replace the old pill-navigation/circle-card experiment.

OWN-WORLD: Lavender matte fields, espresso typography, mauve accents and photographic skin. Bodoni Moda fashion display with Manrope utility voice. Square images, restrained rectangular controls, generous composition, and a consistent accessible focus language.

STORY: See the skin-first offer, browse six products, explore ingredients, find a ritual and assemble a browser-local demo bag. Photography is campaign illustration, not customer evidence.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Flat navigation with centered fashion wordmark. Lavender campaign occupies one continuous stage: a large two-line serif headline, clear collection/finder actions and a three-edit photographic contact sheet at left; an enormous framed portrait and an associated-product entry below it at right. Mobile stacks the contact sheet and photograph. Selected edit, photograph, product and accessible feedback remain synchronized.

FORM: Grounded list ordered: Botanical Colour Studio; Perfume Artbook; The Beauty Atelier (selected position 3); Resort Ritual; Beauty Contact Sheet; Textile Colour Library; Contemporary Salon Menu. Seed 64c4ec74. Six catalog challengers declined on audience identification and product clarity. Their discipline raises: clear product roles, complete keyboard state, stories attached to choices, photographic sequencing, whole-system commitment, and bold hierarchy. No borrowed costumes.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Find your ritual offers two clear preference choices and returns real catalog recommendations; product details let visitors choose applicable shades and persist those choices in the bag. The photographic contact sheet selects three edits and connects each one to its actual catalog essential. Reduced motion keeps immediate, explicit state feedback.

## Motion upgrade

FOCAL MOMENT: Open the atelier once: the portrait frame and two headline lines open in a coordinated editorial aperture, with total first-view choreography capped at 800ms. Content is visible without scripts, and the sequence is skipped after scrolling, under reduced motion, or when the document is hidden.

CONTINUITY: Manual campaign switches reveal the next decoded photo while retaining the prior frame behind it, with latest selection winning interruptions. Native View Transitions explain collection rearrangement; unsupported browsers receive the immediate correct filter.

FEEDBACK: Bag changes highlight the existing quantity control; mobile menu and ritual result arrivals are brief and state-specific. The bag drawer arrives from the edge it occupies. No repeated section reveals or autoplay.

BUDGET: No new runtime dependencies, perpetual loops, scroll listeners, cursor effects or full-page blur. Effects are bounded to the campaign or changed control. Preference changes and document hiding cancel owned motion while preserving the selected state and working controls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
